Welsh-Powell Greedy Coloring Logic:

  • Vertices: Each scheduled class session is a vertex.
  • Conflict Edges: An undirected edge is established between two classes if they share the exact same timeslot (cannot share a classroom).
  • Degree Ordering: Classes are sorted descending by their conflict degree (number of concurrent classes).
  • Greedy Coloring: The lowest available room number (starting at 1) that is not taken by any already-colored neighbor is assigned.
